Chris P. Giles is a creative entrepreneur, live event specialist, and founder of Black Thai Media — a hybrid production agency built for bold brands, visionary creators, and unforgettable moments.

Born in the DMV and now based in Charlotte, Chris got his start over 20 years ago running sound in churches and DJing everything from weddings to warehouse parties. What began with speakers and sermons turned into a coast-to-coast career producing concerts, corporate events, fashion shows, and full-scale brand experiences.

When 2020 hit, Chris pivoted fast — bringing his production chops into the digital space. Today he's a livestreaming creator and content strategist on Amazon Live and YouTube, helping brands and businesses launch with less confusion and more confidence.

The Journey

20 Years.
One Direction.

Early 2000s

Church Sound & The DJ Days

Started running sound in DMV-area churches while DJing everything from weddings to warehouse parties. Built an ear, built a reputation, and never looked back.

2005 – 2015

Coast-to-Coast Production

Grew from local gigs to full-scale national event production. Concerts, corporate experiences, fashion shows, and faith events with clients from DC to Las Vegas.

2016 – 2019

Black Thai Media Founded

Launched Black Thai Media to bring creative content, event execution, and strategy under one roof. Built a team and a reputation for delivering with clarity, culture, and volume.

2020

The Pivot

When live events stopped, Chris moved fast. Took 20 years of production expertise into the digital space — livestreaming, content strategy, and helping brands survive the shift.

2021 – 2023

Creator Economy & Livestreaming

Built a presence on Amazon Live and YouTube as a creator and consultant. Helped entrepreneurs, brands, and new streamers launch with less confusion and more confidence.

2024 – Now

Black Thai X — The Ecosystem

Expanded into a full holding company. Black Thai X now governs Media, Digital, Commerce, and Labs — an interconnected ecosystem built to help messages move from idea to execution to scale.
