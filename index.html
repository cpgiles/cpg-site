The story

Twenty years of running shows.

Before I ever touched a big stage, I had two classrooms: the church sound booth and the DJ booth. One taught me to serve the moment. The other taught me to move a crowd. Both taught me to listen.

That turned into hundreds of events across the country: concerts, conferences, worship nights, fashion shows and brand events. Somewhere along the way I started Black Thai Media.

Then 2020 hit and every stage went dark. So I turned the cameras on myself: Amazon Live, YouTube, podcasts, hundreds of live streams. Here’s what I learned. The instincts that keep a live show alive are the same ones that keep an audience coming back.

Now I’m in Charlotte. I still mix and call shows, and still making podcasts and content. I’m also using AI and automation to help businesses create more content with less lift.

Nobody claps for the audio guy. That's how you know it worked.
